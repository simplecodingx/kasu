📖 Wallet Types Guide
💵 1. Standard Account
Best for physical cash, UPI lite, or everyday checking accounts. Enter a name and an optional Opening Balance (₹). You can also link Saving Goals to Standard Accounts.
🏦 2. Bank Account (Interest)
Ideal for savings accounts that earn interest. Enter your Opening Balance, Interest % (Per Month or Per Annum), and set when it compounds (e.g., every 1 day of every 1 month). Interest is credited automatically on schedule.
💳 3. Credit Card
Quickly tracks card dues and available limit. Just enter your Total Credit Limit and Amount Spent So Far (0 if fresh)—the due date auto-fills 19 days after your statement day, and APR defaults to 3.5%/mo.
🔄 4. Monthly Limit / Allowance
Great for fixed monthly allowances or strict spending caps. Enter a Limit Amount (₹) and a Reset Day (1–28)—your wallet balance automatically refills back to the limit on that day every month.
🔒 Account Liens / Holds
Tap ✎ (Edit) on any wallet (or use the optional lien option when creating one) to place or release a lien/hold. Your usable balance automatically reflects funds locked under lien.
